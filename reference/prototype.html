About DePin.Builders

Most DePIN coverage is a press release with a referral link attached. We built the opposite.

DePIN is one of the few corners of crypto with a real product. Wireless coverage. Centimeter GPS. Weather data. Compute. Storage. Real hardware doing real work in the physical world, coordinated by token incentives instead of a single company. The opportunity is genuine. So is the noise.

The real issue is verification. Anyone can publish a yield number. Very little of it is tied to anything you can check. Saturation kills returns quietly. Emissions paper over weak demand until they stop. The incentives currently reward whoever markets hardest, not whoever ships the most useful network.

DePin.Builders exists to close that gap. We verify projects against a published methodology, test hardware where we can get it, model break-even honestly, and say plainly when a network only works while the subsidy lasts. We make money through clearly disclosed affiliate links, paid placement, and verification services. We never let that decide a score.

Measured, not estimated. That is the whole standard.
